The existing reverse-client-timeout unit now distinguishes a failed partial
frame from a response wait timeout after successful publication: partial writes
must abort the poisoned client, not try to keep it alive by replying only to
the backend. The separate unanswered reverse-response and explicit client
failure contracts still require failed replies to the captured backend.
